How It Works

Seamless Payroll in 4 Simple Steps

We have simplified the complex world of payroll. Our automated process ensures accuracy, compliance, and on-time payments every single month.

01

Sync & Onboard

Integrate your existing HRMS or biometric attendance systems seamlessly. We securely import all your employee data, tax declarations, and salary structures in minutes.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our payroll platform. Can't find the answer you are looking for? Contact our 24/7 support team.

How long does it take to set up my company account?

Setting up your account is incredibly fast. With our bulk data import tool, you can onboard your entire team, sync your existing HRMS data, and be ready to run your first payroll in under 24 hours.

Is my financial and employee data secure?

Absolutely. We use bank-level AES-256 encryption to protect your data both in transit and at rest. Our infrastructure is ISO 27001 certified, and we mandate Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for all administrative access.

What happens if tax laws change mid-year?

You don't have to worry about compliance. Our legal and tech teams constantly monitor government regulations. Any changes in tax slabs, PF, or ESI rules are automatically updated in our core engine without requiring any manual action from you.

Can employees access their own payslips?

Yes! Every plan includes access to our Employee Self-Service (ESS) portal. Employees can log in via web or our mobile app to view current and past payslips, declare tax savings, and download Form 16.

Do you support multiple currencies for global teams?

Multi-currency support is available exclusively on our Enterprise plan. It allows you to process payroll across 50+ countries with automated real-time exchange rate conversions and localized compliance.
